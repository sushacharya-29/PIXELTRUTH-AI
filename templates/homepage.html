Process

From Upload to Verdict
in Under a Second

Pixel Truth runs a multi-layer analysis pipeline using our spatial model, examining patterns invisible to the human eye.

Step 01
Image Ingestion
Upload any image format — JPEG, PNG, WebP. The pipeline immediately unpacks pixel-level data for deep inspection.
Step 02
Spatial Analysis
Our spatial model scans texture regions, frequency patterns, and edge signatures that GAN models typically distort.
Step 03
Verdict Output
A confidence-weighted verdict is returned: AUTHENTIC or SYNTHETIC, with per-layer attribution data for full transparency.

Under the Hood

Spatial Model Architecture

The detection core is a purpose-built spatial network that understands not just what is in an image, but the physics of how it was created.

  • Texture Frequency Analysis
    Examines high-frequency content that GAN upsampling typically distorts, invisible to standard CNNs.
  • Spatial Attention Heads
    Self-attention across image patches captures long-range inconsistencies that localized convolutions miss.
  • Multi-Scale Feature Extraction
    Detects artifacts at pixel, patch, and global levels — ensuring no synthetic signature goes undetected.
